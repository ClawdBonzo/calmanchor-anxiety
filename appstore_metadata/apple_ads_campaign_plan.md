# Apple Ads — CalmAnchor launch campaign (to create manually at app-ads.apple.com)

Creating a campaign commits ad spend, so this is yours to click. ~5 minutes.

## Campaign
- App: CalmAnchor (com.clawdbonzo.CalmAnchor)
- Placement: Search Results · Country: United States (add GB, AU, CA after 2 weeks if CPA is acceptable)
- Name: `CalmAnchor – Panic Intent – US`
- Daily budget: **$8** · Bid strategy: Manage Bids · Default max CPT: **$1.20**
- Search Match: **OFF** (otherwise it spends on generic "anxiety" traffic at $3+ CPT)

## Ad group 1: `Panic – Exact` (max CPT $1.50)
Exact match: panic attack help · panic attack app · stop panic attack · panic attack relief · panic attack breathing · grounding techniques · 5 4 3 2 1 grounding · grounding app · box breathing · anxiety attack help · calm down app

## Ad group 2: `Calm – Broad` (max CPT $0.80)
Broad match: panic relief · grounding exercise · breathing for anxiety · anxiety sos

## Negative keywords (campaign level)
free, game, music, sleep sounds, meditation, therapy, therapist, betterhelp, calm (brand), headspace

## What "working" looks like after 14 days
- Tap-through rate > 8%, conversion (tap→install) > 40%, CPA < $4.
- If CPA > $8 after $100 spend: pause Broad group, keep Exact only.
- Watch RevenueCat for trial starts from ad-installed cohorts; ASC Analytics → Acquisition → Source "Apple Search Ads".
